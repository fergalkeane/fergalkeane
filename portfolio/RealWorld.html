01 — The Challenge

One book of risk, scattered across a dozen disconnected tools.

Underwriters and risk managers tracked marine, property, aviation, energy, cargo and ports exposures across siloed spreadsheets and single-purpose tools. There was no single view of accumulated exposure, and live events — sanctions, AIS silence, severe weather, region breaches — surfaced too slowly to act on.

02 — The Solution

A unified intelligence platform built on one shared map paradigm.

Real World brings every portfolio onto a single live global map with real-time exposure, alerts and deep analytics. Each line of business gets a purpose-built workspace that shares one design language, navigation model and component library — so a marine underwriter and an aviation risk manager feel immediately at home in each other's screens.

03 — Impact

$4.2B of exposure monitored live across six portfolios — with sanctions, weather and breach alerts surfaced the moment they occur.

02.1 — The Product

A guided walk through Real World

From a single sign-in to six specialised risk workspaces, every screen shares the same spatial logic: portfolios on the left, aggregated exposure in the centre, live risk on the right, and the world map below.

01 / Sign In

An end-to-end entry point

A split sign-in pairs a rotating gallery of insured assets with a focused authentication card — SSO and SAML for enterprise, plus a live systems-operational status indicator that sets the tone before a user is even inside.

02 / Command Center

The whole book at a glance

The landing dashboard greets each risk manager with headline KPIs — total exposure, active policies, live risk alerts and global regions — then routes them into Marine, Property, Aviation and Offshore/Energy through large, asset-counted entry cards.

03 / Marine

A live fleet on one map

The marine workspace plots the entire insured fleet on a global chart. Selecting a vessel opens an at-a-glance voyage card — route, speed, draught, flag and policy type — while regional aggregation and a sanctions / AIS-silence alert rail keep accumulation in check.

Live MapAISExposure Aggregation

04 / Vessel Detail

Everything known about a single hull

Drilling into a vessel unifies policy details, port calls, a 0–100 risk score with year-on-year trend, inspection history, environmental CII ratings and a scrubbable AIS track — the deepest view in the platform, organised so nothing competes for attention.

05 / Aviation

From global airspace to a single gate

Aviation reuses the same spatial grammar for a different asset class: portfolio exposure by airport and region, live NOTAMs and risk-area levels, then an airport drill-down with aircraft-on-the-ground, incoming traffic and summary statistics over a satellite view.

06 / Property

Catastrophe exposure, geolocated

The property workspace maps $96B of insured value against high-value markets and live weather risk — wildfire zones, hurricane tracks, seismic zones — so concentration in a cat-exposed region is visible the instant a threshold is breached.

Declarations screened against AIS

Cargo voyage analysis turns an uploaded declarations workbook into a screened portfolio — every vessel checked against AIS for voyage progress and breach of a stated region. Completed, started, pending and breached voyages roll up into a summary, with each route inspectable on the map.
